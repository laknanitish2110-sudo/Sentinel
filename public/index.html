🔐 Why Trust Sentinel?

Sentinel's power isn't any single data source — it's the cross-verification of five independent sources that can't be simultaneously compromised.

01

5 Independent Sources, 1 Truth

The contractor submits the MB record. The bank holds the payment. The supplier has the invoice. The photo is GPS-stamped. The citizen walks the site. No single entity controls all five.

02

Collusion Is Expensive

To fool Sentinel, a contractor would need to falsify the MB record AND forge bank statements AND fake supplier invoices AND doctor geotagged photos AND silence local citizens. That's not one bribe — it's an entire conspiracy across independent institutions.

03

AI Finds — Humans Decide

Sentinel never accuses. It flags discrepancies with evidence, then hands the case to a human auditor. The AI can be wrong — that's why auditor corrections are preserved and the system learns from them.

04

Every Claim Is Traceable

No black boxes. Every finding links to a specific document, a specific measurement, a specific photo. The database is the source of truth — not the AI's opinion.
