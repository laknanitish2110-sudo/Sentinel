Pattern: Staged Material Discrepancy

Previously, Sentinel treated underground pipe work as unexecuted physical progress based solely on trench photos. A human reviewer clarified that contractor staging yard records verify Hume pipes delivered to site awaiting laying. Sentinel now cross-references site delivery bills whenever similar linear trench photo discrepancies appear.

Precedent Rule Established: Check supplier delivery invoices before flagging trench photo discrepancies.
