# Sentinel Revenue Model & Government Partnership Strategy

## Core Business Model: B2G SaaS (Business-to-Government Software-as-a-Service)

Government departments subscribe to Sentinel for continuous, automated monitoring of infrastructure fund utilization. Sentinel is an **advisory intelligence layer** — it provides data-driven risk assessments and flags discrepancies, but the decision authority always stays with the government officer.

---

## Why Advisory, Not Authority

Sentinel does NOT approve or block government spending. It provides **Pre-Sanction Risk Intelligence**.

### The Credit Score Analogy

A credit score doesn't approve or reject your loan — the bank does. But no sane bank ignores the score. Sentinel works the same way for government fund releases:

- Before an officer releases the next installment, Sentinel shows a **risk card**
- The card shows cross-verified evidence from 5 independent sources
- The officer still makes the decision — Release or Hold
- But now they're **informed**, and there's a **record** of what they were shown

This is more powerful than blocking because it creates **accountability without authority**.

### What Sentinel Shows Before a Fund Release

| Signal | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Contractor history | "This contractor's last 3 projects had measurement mismatches averaging 35%" |
| Progress vs. payment | "Site photos show 40% completion but contractor requests 75% payment" |
| Cost benchmarking | "Similar project in neighboring district cost ₹8L — this one claims ₹14L" |
| Document consistency | "Invoice dates don't match treasury release dates by 45 days" |
| Related party flag | "Materials supplier is same entity as the contractor" |

The officer sees all of this before clicking "Release." If they release despite red flags, the system has a record — which creates natural accountability.

### Why This Works Better Than Blocking

1. **Governments will buy it** — it helps them, doesn't threaten their authority
2. **Zero legal liability** — Sentinel provides information, not verdicts
3. **Creates accountability** — officers who ignore red flags are on record
4. **Scales politically** — no legislative change needed, just procurement
5. **Aligns with RTI** — all data is already public; Sentinel just connects it

## Real-World Example: Jhansi District, Uttar Pradesh

### The Setup

- 147 infrastructure projects (PMAY housing, Swachh Bharat toilets, PMGSY roads)
- Total sanctioned budget: ₹82 crore
- Staff: 1 District Collector, 4 junior engineers, 2 clerks
- Current audit coverage: ~1.5% (CAG checks maybe 2 out of 147 projects)

### Without Sentinel (Current State)

- Contractors submit self-reported progress
- Junior engineer visits 8-10 sites/month out of 147
- Clerk downloads PFMS data, prints it, files it — no cross-referencing
- One contractor claims ₹12L for 500m of road. Treasury released ₹12L. MB says 500m
- Actual road: 280m. ₹5.3L unaccounted. Nobody catches it

### With Sentinel (₹3 lakh/year subscription)

**Month 1 findings:**

**Project #47 — PMGSY Road, Block Mauranipur**
- Measurement Book claims: 500m completed
- Treasury released: ₹12,00,000
- Site photo analysis: ~280m visible
- Satellite imagery: partial work, doesn't match claimed area
- **Discrepancy: ₹5,32,000 unaccounted (84% confidence)**

**Project #91 — Swachh Bharat Toilets, Block Babina**
- Supplier invoice: 200 toilet units at ₹8,000 = ₹16,00,000
- Treasury released: ₹16,00,000
- Citizen reports: only 130 toilets visible
- **Discrepancy: ₹5,60,000 for 70 missing toilets (79% confidence)**

**Project #23 — PMAY Housing, Jhansi Urban**
- Contractor invoice dates don't match treasury release dates by 45 days
- Materials supplier = same entity as contractor (related party)
- **Procedural red flag — no amount discrepancy**

### The ROI

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Jhansi pays | ₹3,00,000/year (₹25,000/month) |
| Discrepancies found in Month 1 | ₹10,92,000 |

| **ROI in Month 1** | **2.2x annual subscription** |

## Government Partnership Model

### How Sentinel Integrates (Not Replaces)

```
Current Flow:
  Contractor submits claim → Officer checks (manually, partially) → Releases funds

With Sentinel:
  Contractor submits claim → Sentinel generates Risk Card → Officer reviews card → Releases or holds funds
                                                                         ↓
                                                              Decision recorded with
                                                              evidence context
```

### What Changes for the Government Officer

1. **Before releasing funds** — they see a Sentinel Risk Card with cross-verified data
2. **They still decide** — Release, Hold for Inspection, or Flag for Audit
3. **Their decision is recorded** — with what evidence they were shown
4. **If they release despite red flags** — it's on record (natural accountability)
5. **If Sentinel was wrong** — the correction feeds back into Case Memory

### What Sentinel NEVER Does

- Never blocks or approves fund releases
- Never accuses any individual of corruption
- Never makes legal determinations
- Never replaces the auditor's judgment
- Never shares individual officer decisions publicly

### What Sentinel ALWAYS Does

- Shows evidence from 5 independent sources
- Flags where sources contradict each other
- Provides confidence scores on every finding
- Records what evidence was available at decision time
- Learns from human corrections to improve future assessments
